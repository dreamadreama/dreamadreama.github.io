About

Wallpaper undoubtedly has a transformative effect on our homes and its rejuvenating properties are one of the many things I love about it. Often the wish is to bring nature into our homes with beautiful and hypnotic plant, animal, and bird designs. And sometimes to tie a room together we simply need a splash of color or to introduce a new texture.

I have been installing wallpaper of all kinds, in homes across the metro and beyond, for more than three years. My intense attention to detail brings a meticulousness to installation and removal that is unique to my years spent as an artists’ assistant, and I have every intention of deepening my love of this revered craft.

I suggest homeowners seek wallpapers with minimal environmental impacts and I keep a log of brands that are eco-friendly which I am always happy to recommend. Additionally, I am a member in good standing of the Wallcovering Installers Association.
